About ChronoFlow

ChronoFlow brings everyday time tools into one simple page. Use the live clock, alarms, reminders, countdown timer, stopwatch and focus timer to organize your day.

Privacy

Alarms and reminders are stored locally in your browser. This single-file demo does not include a user account or cloud sync. Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

Terms & usage

Use ChronoFlow as a convenience tool, not as a guaranteed safety-critical alarm. Browser permissions, device settings, power saving, sleep, and closing the page may prevent alarms from firing.
